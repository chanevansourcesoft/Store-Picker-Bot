  1. Put the base where the app says, using the floor marks. Move it only when the app asks.
    2. 
            
  2. Put the leader arm in its rest pose, then press Space (Start) just before you move.
    3. 
            
  3. Pick only the product shown, put it in the basket, and bring the arm back to the rest pose.
    4. 
            
  4. Press N (Save) as soon as the arm is back at rest. Nothing is saved until you press N.
    5. 
            
  5. Press X (Cancel) if the product falls, you take the wrong one, or a person or hand is in the camera view.
    6. 
            
  6. Wait for Saved before the next episode. Meanwhile, put the product back, a little differently each time.
    7. 
            
  7. Move smoothly, at a steady pace, with the same grasp for the same product.
